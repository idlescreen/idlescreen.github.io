IdleScreen v5.0.0 is a capability-secured ambient screensaver and idle daemon suite engineered 100% natively in openOODA (.oo). It packs 53 built-in 16-bit arcade, demoscene, and mathematical scenes, bevelled 3D wordmarks (OPENOODA, SUPERFEDORA, FEDORA, ARCH, or custom text), 8-theme parallax starfields, an interactive TUI director (idlescreen tui), and an event-driven idle daemon into a single unified 339 KB native binary (plus cross-compiled Windows PE32+ idlescreen.exe). Runs anywhere — terminals, Linux desktops (SuperFedora, Fedora, Debian, Arch, Alpine, openSUSE), containers, and systemd/OpenRC/runit/s6/dinit — with 0.00% CPU at idle and a flat 2.62 MiB Peak RSS across 1,000+ frames.
